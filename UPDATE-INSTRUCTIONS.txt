SLC collection update

1. Upload the app, components and public folders from this update into the existing GitHub repository at its root. Commit and wait for Vercel to finish deploying.
2. Open ADD-COLLECTION.sql in TextEdit, copy all text into Supabase SQL Editor and click Run. This publishes the 12 new products. It does not replace existing rows.
3. Open /wardrobe and check Hoodies, Tops, Trousers and Sunglasses. Each item has a product page, wishlist button and fit guide.

Prices, stock and exact sizes were not supplied; the new items remain unpriced and not orderable until configured. Edit them in your existing admin when ready.

The additional boards are shown in The SLC edit; Sunglasses model 1.1 is an additional image on the rimless sunglasses page. Both jumpsuits are excluded.
